# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume competence and good faith; criticise code, not people.
- Give specific, minimal reproductions. "Injection fired twice on X" beats
  "it's broken".
- Respect the project's constraint: it must never make a failed memory service
  into a failed session.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, sustained
disruption, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer through GitHub's private security advisory channel or a
direct message. Reports are handled confidentially; consequences range from a
warning to a permanent ban.
